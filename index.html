How to Play
Place songs in chronological order on a growing timeline. The better you know your music history, the further you'll go.
Standard
  • An anchor song is dealt face-up to start your timeline
  • Place each new song where it fits chronologically
  • Start with 3 hearts — each wrong placement costs one
  • Guess the artist & title before reveal for a bonus heart
  • Reach the win target to complete your run
Hardcore
  • Same as Standard — but one wrong placement ends the game
  • No hearts, no margin for error
4 Options
  • Listen to a song and pick which one it is from 4 choices
  • Correct picks earn a point and build your timeline
  • Start with 3 hearts — wrong picks cost one
Name Guess
  • Listen and guess the artist & title — no timeline involved
  • 1 pt for artist · 1 pt for title · 3 pts for both
  • Start with 3 hearts — guessing nothing correct costs one
Multiplayer
  • Teams take turns placing songs on their own timelines
  • First team to reach the win target wins the match
  • If scores tie at round end, a Tie-Breaker continues until one team leads
